# Task 09 – Pendulum Measurements

## Problem Statement

Create a pendulum simulation and measure the time for 10 oscillations repeatedly.

Using the measurements:

1. Determine the mean period
2. Determine the standard deviation
3. Calculate gravitational acceleration
4. Determine uncertainty

---

## Theory

For a simple pendulum:

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
$$

Rearranging:

$$
g = \frac{4\pi^2L}{T^2}
$$

---

## Experimental Data

Times for 10 oscillations:

$$
20.1,\ 20.3,\ 20.2,\ 20.0,\ 20.4,
$$

$$
20.1,\ 20.2,\ 20.3,\ 20.1,\ 20.2
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Mean time

$$
\bar{t} = 20.19\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

### Step 2: Mean period

$$
T = \frac{20.19}{10}
$$

---

$$
T = 2.019\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

### Step 3: Standard deviation

$$
\sigma \approx 0.12\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

### Step 4: Calculate gravity

Assume pendulum length:

$$
L = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}
$$

---

$$
g
=
\frac{4\pi^2(1.00)}{(2.019)^2}
$$

---

$$
g \approx 9.68\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

---

## Final Result

### Mean period

$$
T = 2.019\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

### Standard deviation

$$
\sigma = 0.12\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

### Gravitational acceleration

$$
g \approx 9.68\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

---

## Interpretation

Experimental values are close to the accepted gravitational acceleration near Earth’s surface.
