# Task 07 – Standard Deviation

## Problem Statement

The following test scores were recorded:

$$
88,\ 92,\ 79,\ 85,\ 95,\ 81,\ 86,\ 90,\ 83,\ 77,\ 89
$$

Determine:

1. Mean
2. Standard deviation
3. New mean and standard deviation after removing highest and lowest scores

---

## Theory

### Mean

$$
\bar{x}
=
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}x_i
$$

---

### Standard deviation

$$
\sigma
=
\sqrt{
\frac{1}{N-1}
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
(x_i-\bar{x})^2
}
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Number of scores

$$
N = 11
$$

---

### Step 2: Sum of scores

$$
88+92+79+85+95+81+86+90+83+77+89 = 945
$$

---

### Step 3: Mean

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{945}{11}
$$

---

$$
\bar{x} \approx 85.91
$$

---

### Step 4: Standard deviation

Using:

$$
\sigma
=
\sqrt{
\frac{1}{10}
\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2
}
$$

---

Calculated result:

$$
\sigma \approx 5.69
$$

---

# Removing highest and lowest scores

Highest score:

$$
95
$$

Lowest score:

$$
77
$$

Remaining scores:

$$
88,\ 92,\ 79,\ 85,\ 81,\ 86,\ 90,\ 83,\ 89
$$

---

### Step 5: New mean

Sum:

$$
773
$$

---

$$
\bar{x}_{\text{new}}
=
\frac{773}{9}
$$

---

$$
\bar{x}_{\text{new}}
\approx 85.89
$$

---

### Step 6: New standard deviation

$$
\sigma_{\text{new}}
\approx 4.26
$$

---

## Final Result

### Original data

$$
\bar{x} \approx 85.91
$$

$$
\sigma \approx 5.69
$$

---

### Without highest and lowest scores

$$
\bar{x}_{\text{new}} \approx 85.89
$$

$$
\sigma_{\text{new}} \approx 4.26
$$

---

## Interpretation

Removing extreme values reduces the spread of the data and decreases the standard deviation.
